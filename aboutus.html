Rodney Homes

Providing Safe, Supportive Homes for young adults

About Us

Rodney Homes is dedicated to providing safe, nurturing, and supportive residential environments for young people across the UK.

We believe every young adult deserves a stable, loving home — a place where they can feel secure, valued, and empowered to reach their full potential. Our homes are staffed by experienced, compassionate professionals who are committed to making a positive difference in young lives.

Our Mission

To create caring and consistent homes that support emotional growth, education, and wellbeing for every young adult in our care.

What We Do

  • Provide 24/7 residential care for young people aged 16–25
  • Support with emotional, behavioural, and complex needs
  • Work in partnership with local authorities, social workers, and health services
  • Offer personalised care plans and therapeutic support
  • Create safe spaces that encourage independence and personal development

Our Approach

Rodney Homes takes a individual-centred approach to care. We focus on building trust, encouraging resilience, and creating opportunities for every young adult to thrive academically, socially, and emotionally. Our teams are trained in trauma-informed practices and provide consistent support tailored to each person's unique needs.

We understand the importance of stability, structure, and kindness — and we work tirelessly to ensure every young person in our homes feels heard, respected, and supported.
